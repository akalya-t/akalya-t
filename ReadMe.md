### Hey, I'm Akalya. 👋

I started out designing logos and brand identities. Somewhere along the way I got curious about the other side — how the things I was designing actually came alive on screen — and that curiosity turned into frontend development. Now I work as a web developer at a Digital Marketing agency, still carrying the designer's eye into everything I build.

I tend to properly sink into whatever I'm building for a while — not just skim the surface of a tool or a project, but actually sit with it until I understand why it works the way it does.  I think it makes the work more honest.

Right now that means React and Tailwind, mostly. Before that it was Figma, brand systems, and a lot of logo work.

Outside of screens — I read a lot, keep a journal, and paint, sketch, and crochet when I need my hands to do something other people can't undo with Ctrl+Z.

Based in India. English and Tamil.
